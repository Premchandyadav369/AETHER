## 3. Venture Funding & Growth Milestones

### Phase 1: Pre-seed Validation (Months 0–6)
- **Target**: $500k from angels & scientific-focused venture funds.
- **Goal**: Launch pilot programs with 3 biotech startup customers to validate prediction accuracy in wet-lab assays.
- **Milestone**: Publish peer-reviewed preprints on bioRxiv.

### Phase 2: Seed Round (Months 6–18)
- **Target**: $3M Seed Round led by Biotech VCs.
- **Goal**: Expand from binding affinity predictions to automated synthesis route suggestions (introducing V7 generation engines).
- **Milestone**: Establish research partnership with a mid-size pharmaceutical firm.

### Phase 3: Series A (Months 18–36)
- **Target**: $12M Series A round.
- **Goal**: Standardize the MLOps active learning loop to continuously ingest lab assays from automated robotic screens.
- **Milestone**: Push 2 lead candidate molecules generated by AETHER-RAMI into Phase I clinical trials.
